## Chicken goulash with green beans (pileći gulaš sa boranijom)

This one-pot dish can replace any lunch in the weekly plan. It is
filling, protein-rich and does not need flour or potatoes.


### Ingredients

-   400 g chicken breast
-   400 g string beans (zuta boranija)
-   1 medium onion
-   2 cloves garlic
-   1 do 2 tbsp masti
-   1 tsp sweet paprika
-   Black pepper and salt (vegetta)
-   250--350 ml water



### Method

1.  Cut the chicken into approximately 3 cm cubes. Season with salt,
    pepper and paprika.
2.  Heat the mast (lard) in a large pot. Add the finely chopped onion and
    cook for 5-7 minutes until soft.
3.  Add the chicken and brown for about 5 minutes.
4.  Add the garlic, sweet paprika and smoked paprika. Stir for about 30
    seconds; avoid burning the paprika.
5.  Add the string beans and bay leaf.
6.  Add 250--350 ml water. 
	- Close and lock the lid.
	- Select High Pressure.
	- Set cooking time to 15 minutes.
	- Once cooking finishes, allow 5 minutes natural pressure release.
	- Carefully release the remaining pressure according to your cooker's instructions.
